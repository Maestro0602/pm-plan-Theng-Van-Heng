# Stakeholder analysis: Realtime Quiz platform

## Priority stakeholders (high influence and high interest)

The two primary stakeholders possessing both high influence and high interest (H, H) are **S01: Head of the IT department (Sponsor)** and **S02: Supervising lecturer (Project Manager)**. They receive the PM's attention first because the Sponsor holds financial authority, approves scope changes beyond the contingency reserve, and signs off on major phase gates (notably the week-7 MVP demo and week-14 pilot), while the Supervising Lecturer directs team execution, enforces academic milestones, and ensures continuous risk mitigation throughout the 14-week lifecycle. Aligning with these two key leaders ensures that strategic priorities remain supported and project blockers are resolved rapidly.

## Veto stakeholder

The primary veto stakeholder is **S08: Data protection officer (Governance)**, who holds high influence (H) and can unilaterally halt the live student pilot and deployment if the platform's handling, encryption, and retention of student identifiers and academic quiz records do not strictly satisfy university privacy and compliance policies.
